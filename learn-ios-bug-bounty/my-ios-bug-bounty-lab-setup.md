# 🧪 My iOS Bug Bounty Lab Setup

I keep this page for the setup steps I forget between devices. Nothing fancy here, just the links that worked for me.

## Getting the IPA

- [Export IPA from iPhone without iTunes](https://youtu.be/XhTLIux8vw8): Hindi video with English subtitles.
- [Export IPA on Windows 11](https://youtu.be/lKWM2ALU7_w): English version of the same process.

## Jailbreak

- [iOS 12.4.1 jailbreak with checkra1n](https://medium.com/@SecureWithMohit/ios-12-4-1-jailbreak-using-checkra1n-ed7674a4d450): old iOS version, still handy for old test devices.
- [Jailbreak iPhone 7 on iOS 14.8](https://youtu.be/d2MrKVdKjzY): walkthrough for a common spare device setup.

## Lab guides

- [Beginner guide to iOS pentesting with Corellium](https://infosecwriteups.com/step-by-step-complete-beginners-guide-of-ios-penetration-testing-with-corellium-2b9e9c6382c2): for when you do not have a jailbroken device.
- [iOS pentesting lab setup](https://medium.com/offensive-black-hat-hacking-security/ios-pentesting-lab-setup-516ec567b424): covers the tools and device setup.
- [Step by step iOS penetration testing guide](https://infosecwriteups.com/step-by-step-complete-beginners-guide-of-ios-penetration-testing-17092c0e0dc7): end to end beginner guide.
