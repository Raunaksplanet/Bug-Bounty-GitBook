# 📖 Reference

Pages I open often when I need a device detail, a testing step or a reminder of how something works.

- [AppleDB](https://appledb.dev/): device, firmware and jailbreak info.
- [iOS 17 security features](https://medium.com/offensive-black-hat-hacking-security/ios-17-security-features-top-20-new-features-of-ios-17-150c9633ba9f): quick overview of what changed.
- [Ivan Rodriguez iOS posts](https://ivrodriguez.com/tag/ios/): archive of his iOS writeups.
- [OWASP MASTG](https://mas.owasp.org/): mobile app security testing guide, iOS section included.
