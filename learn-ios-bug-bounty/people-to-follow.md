# 👀 People to Follow

Following people who actually publish iOS research is faster than reading random articles. Most of them post writeups, tools or talk recordings on X and their blogs.

- [Brandon Azad](https://x.com/_bazad): iOS exploitation research and writeups.
- [Csaba Fitzl](https://x.com/theevilbit): macOS and iOS security research. His blog is at [theevilbit.github.io](https://theevilbit.github.io/).
- [Ian Beer](https://x.com/i41nbeer): iOS kernel research from Project Zero.
- [Ivan Rodriguez](https://ivrodriguez.com/): iOS security research. He also posts on [GitHub](https://github.com/ivRodriguezCA) and [X](https://x.com/ivMobAppSec).
- [Linus Henze](https://x.com/LinusHenze): iOS and macOS security research.
- [Patrick Wardle](https://x.com/patrickwardle): macOS and iOS security tools, talks and writeups.
- [Prateek Gianchandani](https://github.com/prateek147): built DVIA and published a lot of iOS app security material.
- [sandeepvi on Medium](https://sandeepvi.medium.com/): mobile security posts, mostly iOS.
- [Siguza](https://siguza.net/): iOS internals and exploit notes.
